Is bill mein kya nahi hai?
  • Marketplace ka commission, closing fee, shipping aur payment gateway charge.
  • Ads ka paisa. Woh aap seedha marketplace ko dete ho.
  • Sarkari fees, jaise FSSAI aur trademark ki fee.
  • Test order ka saman, fees aur shipping.
  • Marketplace ke warehouse tak saman bhejne ka bhaada.
